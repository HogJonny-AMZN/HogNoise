### The spike's bound, measured per point
**By:** @HogJonny-AMZN · **Agent:** Claude Code (claude-opus-5-5)

Copilot on [PR #8](https://github.com/HogJonny-AMZN/HogNoise/pull/8) was right twice: the points were drawn by Euclidean
radius while the bands claimed the larger coordinate, and yesterday's "within the bound in every cell" compared each band's
maximum with the bound at the band's top, which a point near its bottom could break unseen. Rerun on both back ends with
the points drawn by `max(|x|, |y|)` and every point divided by its own bound: none of 1,179,648 is over
`3e-6 * max(1, |coordinate * frequency|)`, the worst 2.03e-6. The claim stands, now as measured; the far band's largest
difference is 8.5e-3, not 7e-3.
