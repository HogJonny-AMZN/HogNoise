### The warp rules, coordinate-scaled
**By:** @HogJonny-AMZN · **Agent:** Claude Code (claude-opus-5-5)

[The row](../../plan/board/2026-11-21-warp-output-rules-coordinate-scaled.md) was next in the agreed order. Two
options went to @HogJonny-AMZN:

- **A:** reuse the existing coordinate-scaled bound, `scale * max(1, |coordinate| * frequency)`, so the change is one
  branch in `measure_gpu.derive` and a derivation from the committed records;
- **B:** a new bound scaled by the raw coordinate, with a schema change and every adapter measured again.

The agent recommended A, and the owner said "go". At one frequency the two bounds differ by a constant, and every hog
warp case is at 0.01, the frequency the tolerance file's `range` names. So A follows the drift as B would. What A
gives up: a warp rule's number holds at that frequency and no other, which the file already claims for every rule.
This was not in the first recommendation. It surfaced while building, from the row's own text ("scaled by the raw
coordinate, not the noise coordinate"), and is written into `vectors/README.md` and the row.

`derive` now takes a warp rule from `max_normalised`, as it does a noise rule. From the seven `5340a3f` records the
nine warp rules went from absolute 5e-4 to 5e-3 to coordinate-scaled 5e-5 to 2e-4; no other rule moved, and the test
that recomputes the file from its record passes.

The trial, before the pull request: the far-coordinate probe was given an output folder and run again into
`Spikes/far_coordinates/after-warp-rules/` on the 5090's Vulkan and D3D12 and on WARP. Past 32,768 units all 18 warp
outputs pass on all three, at 0.60 to 0.73 of their bound out to 1,048,576, where before none did (up to 250 times).
Inside 32,768 units 1 to 3 points per band still fall past one or two warp outputs' bound: the rare tail
[the fresh-sample bounds](../../plan/board/2026-11-21-gpu-bounds-from-a-large-fresh-sample.md) are next for. Near the
origin the new bound is tighter than the old and passes, so `max(1, ...)` needs no other floor. The control held on
every adapter: 242 probes reproduce the hog set and pass on their own points.

One noise probe changed status, 3D `fns/cellular_manhattan_noiselookup` in [32,768, 262,144), with identical values.
The cause is older: the probe first ran before Metal's record loosened NoiseLookup's tight-bound fraction to 0.999.

The first run of the trial died on `wgpu` not installed (`uv run --no-sync` without the `gpu` group) and was rerun
after `uv sync --group gpu`; no result came from it.

spec still calling the warp rules absolute. The search for stale text had left out `Docs/superpowers/specs/` and
matched one phrasing on one line. `768e00d` fixed both, and a wider search found the fast-track account too. The
owner agreed the lesson as ledger entry P-27: search for the concept in its several wordings across every documents
