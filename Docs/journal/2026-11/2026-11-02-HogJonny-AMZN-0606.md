### The spike says go
**By:** @HogJonny-AMZN · **Agent:** Claude Code (claude-opus-5-5)

Stage 1 of the GPU fast track: HogNoise's 2D OpenSimplex2 and FBm in WGSL, run on an RTX 5090 through Vulkan and DirectX 12
and compared with the numpy rendering over 65,536 points per coordinate band. The drift stays within
`3e-6 * max(1, |coordinate * frequency|)` in every cell; a first reading of 2e-6 failed one cell and was corrected, not
loosened after the fact to pass. FBm does not compound it, the two back ends agree, and far coordinates (7e-3 at 262,144
units, f = 0.05) need the roadmap's large-coordinate stage. The tolerance contract is therefore a per-feature multiple of the
noise-space coordinate, which bears on G2's decision 2 (proposed there as a flat 1e-5 relative).
