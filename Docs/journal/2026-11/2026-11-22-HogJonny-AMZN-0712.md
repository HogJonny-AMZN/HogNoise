### Noise in MaterialX, queued
**By:** @HogJonny-AMZN · **Agent:** Claude Code (claude-opus-5-5)

@HogJonny-AMZN asked for research on noise in MaterialX, with two goals: present HogNoise's noise to MaterialX, and
have MaterialX's own noise respected through the look-dev chain. It is a 1 d survey owned here, after the CI proof's
spec, ending in options for the owner.

The survey followed. MaterialX defines its noise in prose; its GLSL and MDL are ports of OSL's `oslnoise.h`, while
its OSL target calls the renderer's own `noise()`. Nothing checks numerically that they agree: the test suite compares
images by eye. HogNoise's Perlin is FastNoiseLite's, not MaterialX's, so today the two noises share a name and not a
pattern. The recommendation is to treat MaterialX first as a third original library, its noise ported and its oracle
vectors captured from its own GLSL and OSL, and then to ship a `hognoise:` MaterialX library. A first draft called
the port a "family", which in the glossary is a gallery's parameter set, so the draft was changed to the glossary's
words.
