# The GPU fast track: today's noise on wgpu, in WGSL

**Section:** next
**Owner:** @HogJonny-AMZN
**State:** Stage 1 done 2026-11-02 (the spike: go); stage 2, the port, after the conformance harness

[The design](../../design/2026-11-01-gpu-fast-track.md): every current feature on wgpu from one WGSL source, chosen only when
asked for, within a measured tolerance per feature; HLSL, GLSL and Metal generated by naga as the stretch. Three stages: a
spike that measures the float32 difference (1 to 2 d), the port (8 to 12 d), the emitted languages (2 to 4 d). Not blocked by gate G2: that row exempts this one, which ports the current kernels and
needs none of the roadmap's open decisions.

Stage 1 measured: GPU f32 drift within `3e-6 * max(1, |coordinate * frequency|)` on Vulkan and DirectX 12, FBm not
compounding it, far coordinates needing the large-coordinate stage ([the spike](../../../Spikes/gpu_fast_track/README.md)).
Stage 2 waits on the conformance harness by the owner's choice (2026-11-02), so the port is checked by the harness every port uses.
